SmartWalker Privacy Policy

Effective: October 6, 2026

The short version: SmartWalker runs entirely on your iPhone. We don't have servers, accounts, analytics, or ads. We don't collect, see, sell, or share your data. Everything the app stores stays on your device, and deleting the app deletes it.

This policy explains what the SmartWalker iOS app ("the app") uses on your device and why. The app is published by Halycon ("we", "us").

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not send any personal information, photos, audio, maps, health data, or usage data to us or to any third party. There are no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking tools in the app.

Speech recognition

When your iPhone supports on-device speech recognition for your selected language, voice commands are processed entirely on your device. If on-device recognition isn't available for that language, iOS may send the audio of a voice command to Apple to be transcribed. That processing is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy, not by us, and we never receive the audio or the text.

Sharing

We don't share data because we don't have any. If you use a share button in the app (for example, to export a route), the data goes only where you choose to send it.

Backups

Like most apps, SmartWalker's on-device data may be included in your iPhone's iCloud or computer backup if you have backups turned on. Those backups are controlled by you and Apple.

Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove everything it has stored. Apple Health data belongs to the Health app; you can manage or revoke SmartWalker's access in Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices. You can turn off any permission at any time in Settings > SmartWalker.

Children

SmartWalker is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we'll update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.
