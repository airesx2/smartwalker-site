SmartWalker Support

SmartWalker is an iPhone app that gives spoken, hands-free guidance to people with low vision who use a walker. It warns about obstacles ahead, maps your home, and gives voice directions from room to room.

Need help? Email a.y.liang9@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 business days. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version.

Important safety information

SmartWalker is an assistive tool. It is not a medical device and does not replace a cane, guide dog, mobility training, a caregiver, or emergency services.

  • It will not detect every obstacle. Small, low, thin, transparent, moving, or poorly lit objects may be missed. Stairs, steps, curbs, and drop-offs are not detected.
  • Distances and directions are estimates and can be wrong, especially if furniture has moved since your home was scanned.
  • Warnings can be late or missing if the phone is covered, pointed the wrong way, hot, low on battery, or if the app is closed or interrupted.
  • Fall detection can miss falls and can trigger when no fall happened. An emergency call is placed through your iPhone and may require confirmation on screen. Do not rely on SmartWalker as your only way to get help. In an emergency, call 911 or your local emergency number.
  • Indoor use only. SmartWalker is not designed for streets, crossings, or outdoor navigation.
  • Practice with a sighted helper before using SmartWalker on your own, and re-scan your home after moving furniture.

What you need

  • An iPhone with LiDAR (iPhone 12 Pro or Pro Max, or a later Pro model). On other iPhones, mapping and navigation are unavailable and the app will tell you so.
  • iOS 18.2 or later.
  • A way to mount the phone on the walker facing forward, with the camera unobstructed.
  • Optional: an Apple Watch for step and fall data.

Getting started

  1. Open SmartWalker and allow Camera, Microphone, Speech Recognition, and Motion access. The app needs these to work.
  2. Go to Settings > Set Up Home Map. With a helper if possible, walk slowly through your home while the phone scans.
  3. Name each room when asked (for example "kitchen" or "bedroom").
  4. Add an emergency contact in Settings.
  5. Mount the phone on your walker and start a session from the Detection tab.

Voice commands

  • "Take me to the kitchen" — starts turn-by-turn directions to a room.
  • "What's in front of me?" — describes what's ahead.
  • "Describe this room."
  • "I'm actually in the living room" — corrects your location.
  • "Yes" / "No" — answers the fall alert.

Troubleshooting

The app doesn't hear me

Check that Microphone and Speech Recognition are allowed in Settings > SmartWalker, and speak after the listening tone. Background noise can interfere.

The app doesn't know which room I'm in

Say the room you're in. If you're not sure, follow the prompt to turn slowly in a full circle with your walker so the app can match the room.

Directions say "no route"

A doorway may be blocked on the map. Re-scan the room or edit the map in Settings, or ask a helper to check the Live Map.

The phone gets warm or the battery drains fast

Camera and LiDAR use a lot of power. SmartWalker warns you at 20% and 10% battery. Keep sessions short and charge between uses.

Warnings seem wrong after rearranging furniture

Re-scan your home in Settings > Set Up Home Map.
